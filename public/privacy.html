Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

4 man comics is a small hobby project where friends take turns drawing comic panels. This page explains what information the site handles and why.

What we collect

  • If you log in with Google: your email address and name, which Google shares with us through Supabase (our login provider). Logging in is optional, except for starting a comic.
  • Your drawings: every panel you submit, plus which comic it belongs to and, if you were logged in, which account drew it.
  • Comic details: number of players, loops, turn order, status and dates, so the comic can be passed along and finished.
  • Email addresses you type for a friend: used once to send them their turn. We do not store them in our database. Our email provider may keep standard delivery records.
  • Cookies and browser storage: a login session cookie if you log in, and temporary browser storage that holds an unsent drawing while you log in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To pass each panel to the next player and assemble the finished comic.
  • To email logged-in participants when it is their turn, when a comic is finished, canceled or timed out, and to remind a comic’s creator when it has stalled.
  • To keep the site working and secure.

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Who sees your drawings

A player only sees the panel directly before theirs. Once a comic is finished (or ends early), anyone who has its link can view it, so share the link only with people you want to see it. Links are long and random, but they are not password protected.

Services that handle data for us

  • Supabase: database, file storage and login.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Resend: sending emails.
  • Google: sign-in.

Each of these has its own privacy policy and handles data under its own terms.

How long we keep things

A comic that waits more than three weeks on a single turn ends automatically. Comics and their panels are otherwise kept until they are deleted. You can ask us to delete your account information and the comics you created at any time (see Contact).

Your choices

  • You can play without an account if a friend sends you a link, but you will not receive the finished comic by email.
  • You can log out at any time.
  • You can ask to see or delete your information.

Children

The site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
